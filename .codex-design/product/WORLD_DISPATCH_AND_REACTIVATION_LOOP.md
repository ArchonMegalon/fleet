# World Dispatch and Reactivation Loop

## Purpose

This file defines the smallest recurring output that makes the world feel alive and pulls users back into the product without requiring a giant simulation layer.

## Product rule

The world should talk back on a cadence.
The cadence should reactivate players, GMs, and communities through receipt-backed consequence, not through generic marketing copy.

## Weekly dispatch bundle

The first weekly bundle should include:

* one city or campaign ticker
* one faction or world spin item
* one GM-only job or consequence digest
* one player-safe "what changed for me" card
* one optional public-safe recruitment or return prompt

## Why this matters

A user should miss Chummer when they skip a week because:

* the world moved
* their crew state changed
* a hook became actionable
* the next useful action is obvious

## Boundary rule

Dispatch is not generic content marketing.
It is the recurring projection of approved campaign and world truth through inspectable receipts.
