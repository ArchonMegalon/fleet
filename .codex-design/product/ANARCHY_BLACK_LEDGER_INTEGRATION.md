# Anarchy Black Ledger Integration

Shadowrun Anarchy fits Black Ledger because the product loop is consequence-forward:

`World Tick -> public-safe pressure -> dispatch -> mobile/session continuity -> next turn`

Integration rules:
- facts come from the same public-safe Black Ledger receipts and dispatches
- the Anarchy lane may add a rules-light interpretation layer
- it may not invent unsupported facts or free-floating fiction
- it may not expose private campaign or support data

Preview statistics:
- narrative pressure
- faction scene heat
- complication flags
- shadow amp pressure
- debt heat
- chaos index

These are public-safe, receipt-backed interpretations for a rules-light lane. They do not replace the base Black Ledger receipts.
