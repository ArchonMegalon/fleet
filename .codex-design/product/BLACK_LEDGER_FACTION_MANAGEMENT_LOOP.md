# Black Ledger Faction Management Loop

Core loop:

`join or found -> choose perks and flaws -> spend AP -> receive receipts -> tick resolves -> dispatch follows`

Required actions:

- Scout
- Recruit
- Secure District
- Sponsor Package
- Publish Dispatch
- Reduce Heat
- Challenge Faction
- Fortify Safehouse
- Gather Receipts

Action points:

- major charter: `3 AP`
- challenger charter: `2 AP`

Every action needs a receipt.
