# Black Ledger Story Authority Policy

Black Ledger storytelling is authoritative only when:
- the fact packet is derived from Chummer-owned receipts or projections
- the dispatch passes privacy, PII, provider-leak, and sourcebook/IP gates
- the published record is stored as a `BlackLedgerDispatch`

Draft-only lanes:
- `executive-assistant`
- `1min.AI`
- `Syllabbles`
- manual steward draft packets

Draft lanes may:
- propose title/body variants
- compress highlights
- suggest tone-safe wording

Draft lanes may not:
- invent unsupported facts
- publish directly
- outrank the source receipt

Human review status values:
- `optional`
- `requested`
- `required`
- `approved`

Publication authority remains first-party Chummer runtime plus canon.
