# Rafter and Pixefy Provider Boundary

## Role

Rafter and Pixefy are release-proof auxiliaries for Chummer 6. They are not product features, product truth, release truth, roadmap truth, or publishing authorities.

## Rafter

Allowed:
- Security, secrets, and dependency scanning for Chummer repositories.
- Live-site checks for `chummer.run` security, performance, accessibility, SEO, and best-practice regressions.
- AI-ready remediation packets for operators and Codex.

Forbidden:
- Direct publishing or production mutation.
- Release approval by itself.
- Roadmap truth, editorial truth, or product-behavior truth.
- Secret storage outside approved local secret handling.

## Pixefy

Allowed:
- Responsive visual QA across desktop, tablet, and mobile surfaces.
- Device screenshot capture, layout checks, and synced scroll/click inspection.
- Auxiliary accessibility and SEO spot checks.

Required route focus:
- Downloads and status surfaces.
- Support and contact flows.
- Black Ledger public surfaces.
- Newsroom, promo, and faction onboarding pages.
- Public landing and PWA continuity shells.

Forbidden:
- Media rendering or Media Factory publishing.
- Product truth, behavioral proof, or release truth.
- Public publishing or route mutation.
- Provider-owned UI decisions without Chummer-side review.

## Operating rule

Both providers may produce auxiliary evidence only. Any pass from Rafter or Pixefy must be treated as supporting proof that remains subordinate to Chummer-owned tests, screenshots, and operator review.
