# Anarchy Public Copy Policy

Allowed public claims:
- dedicated rules-light ruleset lane
- playable preview
- Black Ledger and dispatch integration
- one-page runner profile
- mobile/PWA-friendly play shell

Disallowed public claims:
- full Shadowrun Anarchy book reproduction
- official sourcebook database included
- ruleset-complete unless the mechanics and proof plane actually support that label

Copy rules:
- say `Shadowrun Anarchy`
- say `dedicated ruleset lane`
- say `playable preview` until deeper mechanics proof exists
- never imply the route is an SR5 toggle
