# Rafter and Pixefy Release QA Boundary

Rafter and Pixefy are auxiliary release-proof systems, not product authorities.

Rafter may provide security, dependency, secret, live-site, performance, accessibility, best-practice, SEO, and remediation evidence to Fleet. It may not publish changes, deploy, own release truth, own roadmap truth, or receive write/admin repository access.

Pixefy may provide public-route responsive screenshot and visual QA evidence to Fleet. It may not render product media, own product truth, inspect private campaign data, or replace human visual review.

Fleet may only treat the combined stack as ready when both provider verifications are verified, both gates pass with fresh evidence, and the final verdict file contains `RAFTER_PIXEFY_QA_STACK_READY`.
