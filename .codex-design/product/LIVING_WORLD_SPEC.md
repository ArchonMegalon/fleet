# Living World

Living World is the governed between-session continuity layer for Chummer’s command-to-fallout loop.

It keeps the watch package, faction command, Runner Passport, and aftermath rail attached to the same turn instead of scattering them into separate concept pages.

## Connected lane

Living World now connects:

- the public-safe watch package
- the signed-in Table Pulse Live inbox
- leader briefing and faction command
- Runner Passport continuity
- governed aftermath return loops

## Boundary

Living World is first-party, governed, and fail-closed. It does not claim autonomous simulation, automatic world truth, or unconstrained off-table authorship.
