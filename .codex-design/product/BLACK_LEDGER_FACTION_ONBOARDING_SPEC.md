# Black Ledger Faction Onboarding

Registered users who open Black Ledger without an active faction allegiance must be routed into the onboarding wizard.

Required behavior:

- one active faction per account
- all current runners inherit the account faction
- all future runners inherit the account faction
- no runner-level override in MVP
- user can join an existing faction
- user can found a Major Faction while slots remain
- user can always found a Challenger Faction

Wizard copy contract:

- `Choose your flag.`
- `Join this faction`
- `Found Major Faction`
- `Found Challenger`

No-noise contract:

- no dead links
- no placeholder buttons
- no `href="#"`
- no generic `Learn more`
