# World Broadcast and Faction Propaganda Cadence

## Purpose

This file defines the smallest repeatable publication cadence that makes BLACK LEDGER feel alive without requiring a grand simulation layer.

## Product rule

The world should talk back through receipts.
Broadcasts are projections of approved truth, not independent fiction.

## Weekly cadence

The first recurring cadence should emit:

* one city ticker
* one faction spin or propaganda item
* one GM-only job digest
* one public-safe media card
* one optional recruitment or announcement packet

## Authority rule

Every broadcast item should trace back to one or more approved objects such as:

* `ResolutionReport`
* `WorldTick`
* `NewsItem`
* `IntelReport`
* `JobPacket`

## Why this matters

The product should feel like a world that remembers, not a repo that stores lore files.
