# JACKPOINT Dossier Spec

JACKPOINT turns campaign intel into:

- dossier cards
- mission briefs
- NPC and contact packets

Public route:

- `/jackpoint`
- `/jackpoint/briefings/{briefingId}.md`
- `/jackpoint/briefings/{briefingId}.json`

Boundary:

- player-safe briefing preview only
- GM-private spoiler variants stay off the public rail

Current state:

- shipped MVP
