# Creator Publication Trust, Moderation, and Compatibility Policy

## Purpose

This file keeps creator publication language honest.
Discovery, moderation, compatibility, and adoption should help people choose safely without turning the shelf into a fake certification system.

## Product promise

Creator publication may show why an artifact is discoverable, whether it is currently moderated, and whether it is compatible with the current Chummer truth.
It may not collapse those into one score.

## Truth order

1. Compatibility posture comes from receipt-backed registry truth.
2. Moderation status comes from the governed moderation and appeals state machine.
3. Trust-ranking posture comes from bounded discovery inputs and only affects discoverability order.
4. Adoption and support analytics stay aggregate and secondary.

## Claim vocabulary

### Compatibility posture

Compatibility posture answers whether the publication currently fits the promoted rule environment, package set, and supported artifact lane.
It is not a popularity score and it is not inferred from moderation.

### Moderation status

Moderation status answers whether the publication is approved, limited, under review, removed, or restored through the governed appeals flow.
It is not an endorsement, a quality badge, or a compatibility guarantee.

### Trust-ranking posture

Trust-ranking posture is a discoverability label.
It may reflect bounded inputs such as recent compatibility health, completion of required metadata, bounded adoption bands, update followthrough, and unresolved support or moderation pressure.
It is not a creator safety verdict, a permanent reputation score, or a promise that every table should install the artifact.

## Required publication labels

Public and signed-in surfaces that show ranking or moderation must also show:

* compatibility posture
* moderation status
* trust-ranking posture as discoverability language, not endorsement language
* reason chips or plain-language drivers for the current ranking posture
* the current receipt-backed or registry-backed review timestamp when applicable

## Forbidden claims

The product may not:

* present moderation approval as proof of compatibility
* present compatibility verification as proof of creator trustworthiness
* present trust ranking as a platform safety certification or social credit score
* expose exact private campaign, table, or player telemetry to justify ranking
* use raw complaint counts, exact install counts, or private moderation notes on public surfaces
* hide unknown compatibility behind warm featured language

## Fallback posture

If compatibility receipts are stale or missing, the product must say compatibility is unknown.
If moderation is unresolved, the product must say review is in progress or limited instead of implying approval.
If ranking inputs are incomplete, the product must fall back to `unranked` or equivalent neutral discovery language.

## Boundary rule

Creator publication language may help people discover and reuse work.
It may not become the system of record for rules truth, moderation evidence, or private campaign analytics.
