# Black Ledger Public Lore Policy

The public Black Ledger seed uses only Chummer-owned fictional material.

Non-negotiable rules:
- No official Shadowrun megacorp names, faction names, logos, maps, sourcebook text, canon NPCs, or canon events on public routes.
- Public routes project only the fictional `emerald-sprawl-prelude` seed.
- Public pages may show aggregate pressure, districts, factions, events, arcs, package pressure, and seeded dispatches.
- Public pages must not render private campaign labels or overlays.
- The public note stays: `Seeded fictional preview and opt-in aggregate only. The Ledger explains pressure, not people.`

Public routes covered:
- `/ledger`
- `/ledger/map`
- `/ledger/dispatches`
- `/ledger/factions`
- `/ledger/turns/1`

Private authenticated routes may attach private-lore overlays, but those labels never project back to public HTML, screenshots, search metadata, route proof, or email digest.
