# Safety Privacy IP Policy

Mandatory gates:

1. privacy
2. PII
3. sourcebook/IP
4. provider/LTD leak
5. support/campaign data
6. fact consistency
7. tone/stigma
8. publication authority

Forbidden public data:

- real user names
- email addresses
- private campaign data
- support case data
- account data
- sourcebook text
- provider/LTD names
- operator internals
- raw prompts

Fail-closed:

- do not publish
- do not email
- record suppressed receipt
