# Community Safety Moderation and Appeals

## Purpose

This file defines the safety and trust lane required before public open-run scale becomes credible.

## Product rule

Open runs, public communities, honors, and world-facing artifacts require governance for bad cases, not just happy-path participation.

## Minimum event families

The first governed event families are:

* no-show or abandonment
* unsafe content or table-conduct incident
* harassment or abusive direct contact
* spoiler leak
* application dispute or bad-faith decline dispute
* GM trust or organizer escalation
* faction-seat or leaderboard gaming
* observer-consent violation

## Required states

Every event family needs:

* report intake
* triage
* evidence posture
* temporary action options
* final decision
* appeal route
* retention and publication posture

## Product boundary

Safety tooling should not become punitive theater.
It exists to make public and community play sustainable and trustworthy.
