# Short Story Templates

Turn dispatch:

```text
# {headline}

{world_name} moved again.

{faction_sentence_1} {faction_sentence_2} {package_sentence}

The Ledger kept the names out and the pressure visible: {stat_summary}. {closeout_sentence}

Source: {receipt_id}. Public-safe {scope_label}; no private table data.
```

Faction dispatch:

```text
# {faction_name} — {short_event_title}

{faction_name} did not {expected_action}; they {actual_action}. {district_or_metric_sentence}

The public ledger shows {metric_name} moving {direction} by {delta}. {package_or_closeout_sentence}

Source: {receipt_id}. Public-safe faction dispatch.
```

Avoid phrases:

- dumbest
- ugliest
- drug addicts
- provider names
- internal operator terms
